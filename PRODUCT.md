# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employers posting and managing job listings. They arrive with a role to fill and want to publish clear, credible openings without friction. Secondary users include recruiters managing company profiles.

## Product Purpose

Django Jobs is an open-source job portal where employers publish openings and candidates discover and apply. Success for the posting flow means an employer can create a complete, trustworthy listing quickly and feel confident publishing it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, trustworthy. Professional without being cold; helpful without marketing fluff.

## Anti-references

- Gradient-heavy hero and form chrome
- Purple-on-white SaaS defaults
- Over-decorated dashboard cards and floating badges
- Dense newspaper/broadsheet layouts
- Placeholder lorem copy on task screens

## Design Principles

- Trust through clarity: every field and section has an obvious purpose
- Calm surfaces: solid colors, steady hierarchy, no decorative noise
- Task first: the form should disappear into posting a job
- Consistency with the existing portal vocabulary (blue accent, Bootstrap structure)
- Honest copy: short instructions, no filler

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on text and controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep labels associated with inputs; errors announced clearly next to fields.
