About Django Jobs

An open-source job portal that helps candidates find roles and employers hire with less friction.

Our mission

We connect people with clear job opportunities and give employers a straightforward way to post openings, review applicants, and keep hiring organized.

What you can do here

  • Search jobs by title and location, then apply in a few steps.
  • Build and export a resume with the resume templates.
  • Post jobs, manage company profiles, and track applicants as an employer.
  • Save favorites and follow application status from your dashboard.
